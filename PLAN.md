### 2.5 Our Work Process (6 steps)
1. **Fill the Form**: You answer the 15 questions and book a free video call whenever it suits you.
2. **Strategy Call**: We go through your answers, clarify your vision, and confirm exactly what you want. Then we sign the contract and start the project.
3. **Creative Planning**: You upload your footage. We study it, build the structure, define the style, and prepare a clear plan.
4. **Editing**: We create the main cut, build the storytelling, then add motion graphics, 3D, and VFX where needed.
5. **Final Polish**: Color correction, color matching, sound design, and music.
6. **Review & Delivery**: You give feedback, we revise, and deliver your final video in all required formats.
